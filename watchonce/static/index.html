Watch Once

Record an expert doing a hands-on task once. Watch Once checks everyone else's attempts and shows each step they skipped, swapped or got wrong, next to the expert doing it right.

Meta's Muse Spark watches the videos and SAM 3.1 outlines the evidence.

1

Teach it once

Upload a video of someone doing the task correctly, ideally saying each step out loud. Up to 15 minutes; for long tasks, record at 720p to keep the file small. Videos are analysed by Meta's Model API and anyone with the link can see them, so keep faces and private details out of shot.

    2

    Check an attempt

    Upload a video of someone else doing the same task. Each step is checked three times independently, and a verdict is shown only when most checks agree.
